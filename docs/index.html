Fuelora

Fuel prices in India and the world, compared

Fuelora is an open-source, personal research project. It shows petrol, diesel, kerosene and crude oil prices for every Indian state and union territory, and for countries around the world, in one place.

What it shows

  • Petrol and diesel for all 36 Indian states and union territories, with a second-source cross-check.
  • Petrol, diesel and kerosene averages for world countries.
  • Crude oil benchmarks (Brent and WTI) in US dollars per barrel, from the U.S. Energy Information Administration.
  • Currency conversion using European Central Bank reference rates.

How it works

The project runs on your own computer, not on a public website. Prices are fetched locally and shown in a page served from your machine.

git clone https://github.com/code-kasha/fuelora.git
cd fuelora
./start.sh        # macOS and Linux (not yet tested)
start.ps1         # Windows PowerShell

Requires Python 3.10 or newer. The start script sets up its own environment and opens the page in your browser.

Data sources

  • India petrol and diesel: Goodreturns and Cardekho.
  • World petrol, diesel and kerosene: GlobalPetrolPrices.
  • Crude oil: U.S. Energy Information Administration (free API key).
  • Currency: European Central Bank.
Please note. This is not an official source of fuel prices. Figures come from third-party sites and can differ from pump prices, oil company tables, and each other. The terms of use of the third-party sites have not been reviewed for this project. Check them before reusing any data.
